TOPIC: Chunking

DEFINITION

Chunking is the process of splitting large documents into smaller sections before generating embeddings.

Chunking improves retrieval quality.

---

WHY CHUNKING IS NEEDED

Embedding an entire book into a single vector is ineffective.

Instead:

Document

↓

Chunk 1

Chunk 2

Chunk 3

↓

Embeddings

---

CHUNK SIZE

Chunk Size controls how much text is stored in each chunk.

Small Chunks:

Better precision

Less context

Large Chunks:

More context

More noise

---

CHUNK OVERLAP

Overlap repeats content between neighboring chunks.

Purpose:

Preserve context across chunk boundaries.

Example:

Chunk 1:
Sentence A
Sentence B

Chunk 2:
Sentence B
Sentence C

INTERVIEW QUESTIONS

Q: Why is chunking important?

A: It improves retrieval quality and relevance.

Q: What is chunk overlap?

A: Shared content between neighboring chunks.

Q: What happens if chunks are too large?

A: Retrieval becomes noisy.
