TOPIC: Embeddings

DEFINITION

Embeddings are numerical vector representations of text.

They capture semantic meaning rather than exact wording.

Similar text produces similar vectors.

---

EXAMPLE

Sentence:

What is Machine Learning?

↓

Embedding Model

↓

[0.12, -0.44, 0.89, ...]

---

WHY EMBEDDINGS MATTER

Embeddings enable:

- Semantic Search
- Similarity Search
- Recommendation Systems
- RAG Systems

---

SEMANTIC SIMILARITY

Example:

What is Machine Learning?

Explain Machine Learning.

These sentences have different words but similar meanings.

Embedding vectors will be close together.
