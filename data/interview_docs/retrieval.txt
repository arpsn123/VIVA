TOPIC: Retrieval

DEFINITION

Retrieval is the process of finding relevant information from a knowledge base.

Retrieval is a core component of RAG systems.

TOP-K RETRIEVAL

The retriever returns the K most relevant results.

Example:

k = 3

Returns the 3 most similar chunks.

---

RETRIEVAL QUALITY

Retrieval quality depends on:

Knowledge Base Design

Chunking Strategy

Embedding Quality

Vector Search Quality

---

RETRIEVAL IN VIVA

VIVA retrieves relevant interview knowledge before evaluating answers.

This improves factual grounding.
