INTERVIEW QUESTIONS

Q: What are the main stages of a RAG pipeline?

A: Chunking, embeddings, vector storage, retrieval, context injection, and generation.

Q: Where are embeddings generated?

A: After chunking and before vector storage.

Q: What happens after retrieval?

A: Retrieved context is inserted into the prompt.
